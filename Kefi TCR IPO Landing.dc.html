A STEALTH-MODE HOLDING ECOSYSTEM

Kefi B.V. is an Amsterdam-based investment and holding company with nearly a decade of institutional involvement in international motorsport and automotive-adjacent ventures. As a foundational early-stage investor and leading equity partner across its portfolio, Kefi B.V. has played a formative role in scaling ambitious sporting and commercial platforms from inception to global reach.
